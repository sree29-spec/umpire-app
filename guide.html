Quick start

  1. Open the app link in your phone’s browser (Chrome works well).
  2. On the Score tab, scroll to the bottom and pick the Match length (for example 20 overs).
  3. In At the crease, type the names of the two batters and the bowler.
  4. For each ball, tap the runs (0 to 6). That’s it. The score, overs and over balls update on their own.

Tip: add the page to your home screen (browser menu, then “Add to Home screen”) so it opens like an app. Your match is saved on your phone, so closing the page doesn’t lose it.

Scoring a ball

The top card shows score/wickets, overs (for example 3.2 is three overs and two balls), extras, and the balls of the current over. Below it, choose the type of ball, then tap the runs.

RunsTap 0 to 6Counts as a legal ball. Runs go to the batter.
WideTap Wide, then +0 or the extra runs run1 penalty run is added for you. It’s not a legal ball, so it’s bowled again.
No ballTap No ball, then +0 or the runs off the bat1 penalty run added. Not a legal ball. Starts a free hit.
Bye / Leg byeTap Bye or Leg bye, then the runsCounts as a legal ball and as extras, not as the batter’s runs.
WicketTap OUTCredits the bowler. A new batter comes in.
Run outTap Run outPick runs completed (0 to 4), then who is out. Not credited to the bowler.
  • Undo last ball removes the most recent ball. You can tap it several times.
  • New innings clears the score. Tap it twice (the first tap asks you to confirm). Bowler names are kept.
  • The innings ends automatically at 10 wickets or when the chosen overs are complete.
Run out is only available on a normal ball. For a run out on a wide or no ball, record the ball normally and note the dismissal on your own card.

Batters and bowlers

  • At the crease shows the striker (★) and non-striker with runs and balls faced. Type in the name boxes to rename them.
  • Strike swaps automatically on odd runs and at the end of each over. If it’s wrong (for example after a run out), tap Swap strike.
  • Choose the current bowler from the dropdown, or tap + New to add one. Their figures show as overs-runs-wickets.
  • Bowler max (near the bottom) sets the most overs one bowler can bowl. Type a number, or 0 for no limit.
  • The Overs tab shows the batting card, bowling figures and every over ball by ball.

Banners you’ll see

FREE HIT After a no ball, until the next legal ball. A wide doesn’t end it. The OUT button is switched off, because only a run out (and a few rare dismissals) can get the batter out. Can be turned off with the Free hit checkbox.

Sharing a match live

Two umpires, or an umpire and a scorer, can follow the same match on different phones. You don’t need to sign in to score alone on your own phone. You do need a Google account to share.

The scoring umpire

  1. At the bottom of the Score tab, tap Sign in with Google.
  2. Tap Start shared match. You get a 5-character code. Tell it to the others.
  3. To let a co-umpire score too, type their Google email under Co-umpires who can score and tap Add. Use Remove to take access away.

Everyone else

  1. Open the app, tap Sign in with Google.
  2. Type the code and tap Join.
  3. If you’re a listed co-umpire, you get the scoring buttons. Otherwise you’re in watch mode: the score updates live, and nothing can be changed.
Only one person should score at a time. If two people tap at the same moment, the last save wins and a ball could be lost.

Tap Leave to go back to your own copy of the match. Tap Sign out when you’re done on a shared phone.

Rules tab

Type a word such as lbw, no ball, powerplay or signal in the search box to find a short summary. It covers extras, dismissals, boundaries, dead ball, unfair play and common umpire signals.

These are quick summaries, not the official text. For anything disputed, follow the current MCC Laws of Cricket and the match’s playing conditions.

Questions

Will I lose the match if I close the page?

No. It’s saved on your phone. It stays until you tap New innings or clear the browser’s site data. A different phone or browser starts empty, unless you’re in a shared match.

Does it work without internet?

You need a connection to open the page. Once it’s open, scoring on your own works, but live sharing needs a connection. Open it before you get to the ground.

I scored the wrong ball.

Tap Undo last ball and re-enter it.

The wrong batter is on strike.

Tap Swap strike in the At the crease card.

Sign-in doesn’t work.

Allow pop-ups for the page, and open it in Chrome rather than inside another app (such as a chat app). If the message shows an error code, send it to whoever manages the app.

Why can’t I score in a shared match?

Only the person who started the match and co-umpires they added can score. Ask them to add your Google email.

Umpire’s Companion is a scoring aid. The umpires’ decisions and the official scorebook always take priority.
